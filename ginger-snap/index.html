Ginger Snap

25' Batch · Lacto Fermented

Heat

Lacto-fermented hot sauce that deepens over time. Ginger adds a zesty snap while mellowing into rich warmth. As fermentation builds natural acidity, a final splash of vinegar sharpens the flavor, enhancing spice and complexity. The result: a fiery, tangy sauce with layers of heat that evolve with every drop.

Flavor Profile

Bold Ginger. Slow Heat. Sharp Finish.

First Hit

Bright ginger snap up front. Sharp, clean, immediate. The fermentation gives it a tangy edge that vinegar alone can't produce.

Mid Palate

Chili heat builds steadily. Dark spice undertones rise. The ginger mellows into a warm, rounded backbone that carries the heat forward.

Finish

Long, layered burn. The vinegar brightens the tail end while fermented complexity lingers. Heat that evolves rather than fades.

Character

This one has a subtle bite, and comes out quick. Fiery and tangy with real depth. Not a one-note sauce.

What's In It

Ingredients

Fresh Ginger
Fermented Chili Peppers
Dark Spices
Vinegar
Sea Salt

Every ingredient is lacto-fermented separately in glass jars at 2.9% salt by weight. The individual ferments run for weeks, developing natural acidity and complex flavor before being blended together. A final splash of vinegar locks the profile and brings the pH well below 4.0.

The Process

Brine. Ferment. Blend. Bottle.

Fresh ginger and chili peppers are grown in southern New Hampshire and harvested at peak ripeness. Each component is brine-fermented individually in glass vessels. The slow lacto-fermentation process converts sugars to lactic acid, developing complex sour depth that vinegar-based sauces can't match. After weeks of monitored fermentation, the components are blended, pH-tested, finished with vinegar, and hand-bottled in small numbered batches.
